# Privacy Policy — Smart Timetable Assistant

*Last updated:* October 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Smart Timetable Assistant is an educational project designed to help users organize class schedules and manage timetable information.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you choose to use, the application may process:
- Account information, such as your email address, for sign-in.
- Timetable information, including subjects, days, times, teachers, and rooms.
- Google Calendar event information when you connect your Google account.
- Event details you choose to create in Google Calendar.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information to provide account access, display and manage timetables, and support Google Calendar integration.

## 4. Google Calendar Access

If you connect Google Calendar, the application requests permission to access the calendar information required by its enabled features. Calendar information is used to provide timetable import and event creation features. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

## 5. Storage and Security

The application uses its configured authentication and storage services to support its features. We take reasonable steps to protect information, including restricting access to stored authentication data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, such as authentication, hosting, database, and Google Calendar services.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed to provide the application or meet applicable obligations. Users may stop using the application and revoke its Google account access through their Google Account permissions. For account or stored-data deletion requests, contact the developer using the contact details provided with the application.

## 8. Children's Privacy

This application is an educational project and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The latest version will be published at this page.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information provided with the application.
